# civil-design-standards

KDS — Korean Design Standards (한국설계기준) full-text by clause.

Covers:
- KDS 11 (지반·터널·옹벽·내진)
- KDS 14 20 (콘크리트구조)
- KDS 14 30 (강구조 설계)
- KDS 14 31 (강구조 시공)
- KDS 17 10 (내진)
- KDS 21 (가시설)
- KDS 41 (건축구조)

Quantitative criteria: phi factors, fy/fck limits, anchor lengths, retaining-wall safety factors, etc.
